**Before you go**

- **Weather**: October highs around 21°C and lows around 15°C, mild with some rain days ([Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/m/143166/10/Average-Weather-in-October-in-Matsuyama-Japan)). This is the change of season, so plan for allergies (see 2).
- **Events**: The **Niihama Taiko Festival** runs Oct 16–18, 2026, about an hour away by limited express ([Japan Travel](https://en.japantravel.com/ehime/niihama-taiko-festival/62184)). It's optional on Day 2. Expect crowded trains that weekend. Mon 10/19 is not a Japanese public holiday.
- **Entry**: Visa-free for Korean passport holders, up to 90 days. Visit Japan Web is optional but speeds up arrival; each of the 4 travelers registers about 2 weeks ahead ([Heymondo](https://heymondo.com/blog/japan-travel-requirements-2026/)).
- **Currency**: Yen, ¥1 ≈ ₩8.7 ([kgosu, 2026-09-10](https://www.kgosu.com/2026/09/2026-9-10.html)). Cards are widely accepted in the city; carry cash for small shops, trams, temples and the brewery shop.

## 6. Itinerary (Itinerary 계획)

The early arrival (08:35) and afternoon departure (15:50) give two full days and two half days. Each day mixes one main sight, time in places locals actually use, and a food or drink stop.

### Day 1: Sat 10/17 · Castle hill and Ōkaidō

- **09:15–10:00**: Taxi to Candeo and leave bags.
- **10:00–10:45**: **Bansuisō** (萬翠荘), a 1922 French-style villa on the castle slope, 5 minutes' walk. Open 9:00–18:00, adults ¥400. **Closed Mondays**, so it has to be today ([official](https://www.bansuisou.org/information/)).
- **11:00–13:00**: **Matsuyama Castle**, one of Japan's 12 original castle keeps. Walk up from Bansuisō, or ride the ropeway from Ropeway Street. Ropeway 8:30–17:30; keep 9:00–17:00. Ropeway round trip plus keep: ¥1,040 combo (≈₩9,000) ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5501.html), [Japan Activity](https://japanactivity.com/matsuyama-castle-guide)).
- **13:00 lunch**: **Taimeshi** at Akika on Ropeway Street (see 7).
- **14:00–16:00, local life**: Walk the Ōkaidō and Gintengai covered arcades on a Saturday afternoon. Go down to the **depachika** food floors of Matsuyama Mitsukoshi (on Ōkaidō) or Iyotetsu Takashimaya (Matsuyama City Station) to see what local families buy: mikan in season, jakoten, Ehime sweets.
- **16:00, free block**: Check in and rest, or use the SkySpa. It's been a very early start.
- **18:00 dinner**: **Yakiniku** (see 7).

### Day 2: Sun 10/18 · Dōgo Onsen

- **Early morning, local life**: Take the tram to Dōgo. **Dōgo Onsen Honkan** opens at 6:00 with the *toki-daiko* drum. Kami-no-Yu bath about ¥460, or ¥1,250 with the 2F Botchan-no-ma room; **verify**, as Ehime raised the public-bath price cap in March 2026. For a bath among locals, try **Tsubaki-no-Yu** next door (¥500), the neighborhood bathhouse ([Dōgo official](https://dogo.jp/en/honkan.php), [dogo.jp news](https://dogo.jp/news/4279.html)).
- **Breakfast**: Back at the hotel buffet, or a café in Dōgo.
- **10:00–12:00, local drink**: **Minakuchi Shuzō** (水口酒造), Dōgo's only sake brewery, founded in 1895, makers of *Nikitatsu* sake and Dōgo Beer. The shop *Nikitatsu Kurabu* offers **free tastings 10:00–17:30** ([iyokannet](https://www.iyokannet.jp/spot/1980)). Then stroll Dōgo Haikara-dōri and the Botchan Karakuri clock, and climb to Isaniwa Shrine.
- **12:00 lunch**: **Dōgo Bakushukan** (道後麦酒館) next to the Honkan. Draft Dōgo Beer and Nikitatsu with Ehime dishes ([GLTJP](https://www.gltjp.com/en/directory/item/14702/)).
- **Afternoon, free block**: Walk or take the bus to **Ishite-ji**, temple 51 of the Shikoku pilgrimage, where you'll see *henro* pilgrims in white. Or rest at the hotel. Also check whether the **Machi no Mannaka Nichiyō-ichi** (街のまんなか日曜市, a downtown Sunday market) runs this weekend (**verify**, [Matsuyama City](https://www.city.matsuyama.ehime.jp/kanko/event/sonota/matinomannaka.html)).
- **18:30 dinner**: **Sushi** (see 7).

**Option instead of the Day 2 afternoon: Niihama Taiko Festival (final day).** Take the JR limited express *Shiokaze* or *Ishizuchi* from Matsuyama Station to Niihama (about 1 hour). You'd see the huge drum floats and be back for the sushi dinner. Reserve 4 train seats. Decide by 10/14.

### Day 3: Mon 10/19 · Ōkaidō and home

- **Breakfast**: Hotel buffet. **Check out by 11:00** and leave bags at the front desk.
- **09:30–10:45, local life**: Watch Monday-morning Matsuyama from the tram, then browse around Matsuyama City Station and Gintengai: commuters, shopkeepers opening up. Buy omiyage and local sake: Ichiroku tart, Botchan dango, mikan products, Nikitatsu.
- **11:00 lunch**: **Nabeyaki Udon Asahi**. Open 10:00–16:00, closed Tue/Wed, so Monday is fine. Go early; it closes when sold out (see 7).
- **12:00–13:15**: Coffee near the hotel, then collect your bags.
- **13:15**: Taxi to Matsuyama Airport; arrive by 13:50. Try the mikan juice tap. **15:50** flight 7C1704.
