**Before you go**

- **Weather**: October highs around 21°C and lows around 15°C, mild with some rain days ([Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/m/143166/10/Average-Weather-in-October-in-Matsuyama-Japan)).
- **Events**: The **Niihama Taiko Festival** runs Oct 16–18, 2026, about an hour away by limited express. Its 3-tonne drum floats are carried by 150+ men ([Japan Travel](https://en.japantravel.com/ehime/niihama-taiko-festival/62184)). It's optional on Day 2. Expect crowded trains that weekend. Mon 10/19 is not a Japanese public holiday.
- **Entry**: Visa-free for Korean passport holders, up to 90 days. Visit Japan Web is optional but speeds up arrival; register about 2 weeks ahead ([Heymondo](https://heymondo.com/blog/japan-travel-requirements-2026/)).
- **Currency**: Yen, ¥1 ≈ ₩8.7 ([kgosu, 2026-09-10](https://www.kgosu.com/2026/09/2026-9-10.html)). Cards are widely accepted in the city; carry cash for small shops, trams and temples.

## 6. Itinerary (Itinerary 계획)

Relaxed pace: two or three main activities a day, with one free block each day. Everything is close to the hotel or one tram ride away, so no long day trips.

### Day 1: Sat 10/17 · Ōkaidō and Castle hill

- **Late morning**: Land at MYJ, take the limousine bus to Ōkaidō, and leave bags at Candeo.
- **Lunch**: **Taimeshi** (sea-bream rice) on Ropeway Street, a 5-minute walk.
- **Afternoon**: **Matsuyama Castle**, one of Japan's 12 original castle keeps. Take the ropeway or chairlift from the foot of Ropeway Street. Ropeway 8:30–17:30; keep 9:00–17:00, last entry 16:30. Ropeway round trip plus keep: ¥1,040 combo (≈₩9,000) ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5501.html), [Japan Activity](https://japanactivity.com/matsuyama-castle-guide)).
- **Late afternoon, free block**: Check in, then SkySpa at sunset.
- **Evening**: Pizza dinner (see 7) and a stroll through the Ōkaidō and Gintengai arcades.

### Day 2: Sun 10/18 · Dōgo Onsen

- **Morning**: Tram from Ōkaidō to Dōgo Onsen (about 15 min). Bathe at **Dōgo Onsen Honkan**, Japan's oldest onsen bathhouse, fully reopened in 2024 after restoration. Go early to avoid the crowds. Kami-no-Yu bath ¥460; with the 2F Botchan-no-ma room and corridor ¥1,250 (≈₩10,900); with the Yūshinden imperial chamber ¥1,500. The 3F Tama-no-Yu private rooms need reservations ([official](https://dogo.jp/en/honkan.php), [GLTJP](https://www.gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11360/)).
- **Late morning**: Botchan Karakuri clock, Dōgo Shōtengai (Haikara-dōri) shopping street, Isaniwa Shrine.
- **Lunch**: In Dōgo (see 7).
- **Afternoon, free block**: Walk or take the bus to **Ishite-ji**, temple 51 of the Shikoku 88-temple pilgrimage. Or rest at the hotel.
- **Evening**: Izakaya dinner with Ehime fish and local sake (see 7).

**Option instead of the Day 2 afternoon: Niihama Taiko Festival (final day).** Take the JR limited express *Shiokaze* or *Ishizuchi* from Matsuyama Station to Niihama (about 1 hour). You can see the huge drum floats in the afternoon and be back for dinner. Expect crowds and reserve seats on the train. Decide by 10/14.

### Day 3: Mon 10/19 · Ōkaidō and home

- **Morning**: Breakfast buffet, then **Bansuisō** (萬翠荘), a 1922 French-style villa on the castle's slope, a 5-minute walk.
- **Lunch**: Matsuyama-style **nabeyaki udon** (see 7).
- **Afternoon**: Check out, pick up omiyage (Ichiroku tart, Botchan dango, mikan products), then take the limousine bus to the airport. Try the mikan juice tap before the flight.
